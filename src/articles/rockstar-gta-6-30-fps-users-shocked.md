Rockstar Games has confirmed that Grand Theft Auto VI is aiming to run at 30fps on consoles. The game developer revealed the information in a [Newswire post](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/89834o94713o21/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-now) on November 10 confirming that pre-loading is available for those who have digitally pre-ordered.

Rockstar explains that the game was developed for current gen consoles and that it pushes "both platforms to their absolute limits." However, there is no mention of a 60fps version on any console.

## What the PS5 Pro gets

The upgraded PlayStation 5 Pro doesn't offer an increased frame rate, but Rockstar says that it features "improved clarity" thanks to Sony's PSSR upscaling technology, plus ray-traced shadows that are only available on that console.

## Other info

PS5 features haptics on DualSense controllers, Tempest 3D Audio and quicker loading times. Rockstar says this makes the game "play best on PlayStation 5 consoles."

Xbox will also have support for Xbox Cloud Gaming, meaning that the game will be playable on Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One. It will use the Xbox Series S graphical settings.

Both consoles will feature Remote Play support.
